// PhD Student · Tsinghua University

I am a Ph.D. student in the College of AI at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Yang Liu and Prof. Peng Li. I completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science at Tsinghua in 2025.

I care about the human experience of AI agents (体感). My goal is to build agents that understand what people need, collaborate naturally, and deliver useful outcomes. My research explores outcome alignment, shared understanding, and agent–environment interaction, with a focus on reducing the effort it takes to turn an intention into a useful result.
